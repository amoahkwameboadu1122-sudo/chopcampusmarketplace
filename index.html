Safety on campus

Deal smart, stay safe

Meet in public campus spots. Main gate, lecture blocks, library or the food court — never a private room.
Inspect before you pay. Power on electronics, count pages, test the charger, check fabric quality.
No advance payment. Never send MoMo for an item you haven’t seen. A legitimate seller will agree to meet.
Keep chats in WhatsApp. Screenshot suspicious behaviour and use the report button — we act on every report.
Chop does not process payments, hold funds, or guarantee transactions. Every deal is arranged directly between buyer and seller — the same way you’d arrange it in person.
